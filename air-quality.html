WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS
AQI is a signal.
Air is the reality.

AQI compresses multiple pollutant measurements into a public-facing index. Looking at the underlying pollutants — especially fine particles such as PM2.5 — gives a more useful picture of exposure.

01

PM2.5 matters

Fine particles are small enough to be inhaled deep into the respiratory system.

02

Pollution varies

Season, weather, location and local sources can change air quality significantly.

03

Indoor air matters

Outdoor pollution can enter buildings, while indoor activities can add their own particles.

04
